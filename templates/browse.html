Browse the Templates

Every template is a stand-alone HTML page. Open one to read it, or download the whole set and copy the templates folder into your project. Yellow TODO and TIP boxes are instructions for you to delete, text in ALL CAPS is to be replaced, and example text carries a coloured rule on its right-hand edge. The FAQ explains the conventions in detail.

The same templates are listed three ways below; pick whichever suits how you think about a project. Indented entries are worksheets attached to the document above them.

Getting the templates

  • Download the template archive as a zip, or clone the repository. The templates live in the templates folder and need nothing but a browser.
  • Read the recommended tools before editing, and how to upgrade if you have documents made from an earlier release. The Fili.ReadyTemplate 0.1.0 release notes list what changed since ReadySET 0.9.3, the last Tigris.org release.
  • The original project's words of wisdom, background essays linked from each template, are preserved in the Internet Archive.
